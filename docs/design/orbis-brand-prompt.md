# Orbis — Brand & Design System

Reference: https://claude.ai/artifact/S4TKVxBGQ3Mzzhqp1NZrrk#page-03174c151310 (Orbis Logo Family)

**Identity**
- Name: Orbis
- Tagline: Your world, intelligently connected
- Logo: Conic-gradient ring from `#5BB4E8` → `#42CCA8` → `#3DC87A`, with an inner hole at 45% diameter. Use nested divs, never SVG strokes.

**Colors (Dark-first)**
- Background: `#0E0E10`
- Surface 1: `#141416` | Surface 2: `#1A1A1E`
- Border: `#242428`
- Teal: `#42CCA8` | Blue: `#5BB4E8` | Green: `#3DC87A`
- Red: `#F26B6B` | Gold: `#F0B429`
- Brand gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #5BB4E8, #42CCA8)`

**Typography**
Use Google Fonts **DM Sans** (300, 400, 500, 600). Display: 28–32px/600; headings: 18–20px/600; body: 14–15px/400; labels: 11–12px/500, uppercase, 0.5px letter spacing.

**UI Standards**
- Phone frame: 320px wide, 40px radius, `#0A0A0C` background, 1.5px `#242428` border.
- Cards: `#141416` or `#1A1A1E`, 16px radius, 1px border.
- Pills: 20px radius with teal, blue, red, or gold accents.
- Bottom navigation: 60px high, five tabs—Home, Health, Money, AI, Settings. Use 20px icons, 10px labels, and a teal gradient for the active tab.

**Screen Consistency**
- Home: market tickers, weather/calendar, top story, health nudge, notifications.
- Health: score ring, sleep stages, activity rings, HR, VO2, SpO2, temperature.
- Money: net worth, portfolio donut, budgets, market indices, AI insights.
- AI: gradient user bubbles, dark AI bubbles, quick actions, morning digest, capability cards.
- Email: dark hero, score row, market grid, CTA, footer.

**Brand Assets**
Maintain consistency across logo lockups, app icons, gradient headlines, browser/phone/email/social mockups, navigation, notifications, and loading animations.

**Golden Rule:** Apply this brand system automatically to every Orbis design, screen, component, and asset. Treat these specifications as the single source of truth. Never ask me to repeat them.
